Understanding The Visa

What is the J-1 Exchange Visa?

The J-1 Visa is a non-immigrant visa issued by the United States to approved exchange visitors. At Moore Talented, we secure sponsorships and placements across four distinct categories.

Interns

For current university students or those who graduated within the last 12 months seeking practical experience in their academic field.

Duration Up to 12 Months

Trainees

For professionals with a degree and 1 year of experience, or 5 years of relevant occupational experience without a degree.

Duration Up to 18 Months

Work & Travel

For full-time university students looking to work seasonal jobs in the U.S. during their official summer break.

Duration 3 to 4 Months

Camp Counselor

For youth workers, students, or teachers wanting to share their culture with American youth at summer camps.

Duration Summer Season
