Frequently Asked Questions

Are there hidden visa processing fees?

No. Our Enterprise and Growth plans include the facilitation of the DS-2019. The actual SEVIS and Embassy fees are typically paid by the candidate or subsidized depending on your specific hiring agreement.

Can I cancel my Gold Subscription at any time?

Yes, if you choose the monthly billing option, you can cancel or pause your subscription at any time. Annual contracts receive a 20% discount but require a 12-month commitment.

How accurate is the AI Candidate Match?

Highly accurate. Our engine analyzes 40+ data points including past experience, English proficiency, visa readiness, and cultural aptitude scores to ensure a 92% retention rate.
